Wireless network HUD port

Scope: item-free own-team EU balance, instant net EU/t and rolling five-minute average.
Default P toggles the HUD; Home opens its drag editor and HUD settings.
The separate settings hotkey is initially unbound.
Settings are client-local under MainConfig.wirelessMonitor; no server config sync.
Text/card/curve, displayed fields, voltage tiers, scientific notation, scale,
width, refresh interval, rainbow palette, speed, bold and italic are configurable.
The curve shows balance history collected during the current enabled session.
