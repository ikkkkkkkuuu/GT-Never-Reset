GT-Never-Reset multiblock base, parameter modules and automatic tooltips

Parameter hatches, recipes, overlays and recipe transaction handling are adapted
from the adjacent GT-Not-Good checkout (HEAD
048fd1963de8c6458ef6f800c411be0087cbafdb; local source hashes are recorded in
SOURCE_MANIFEST.json). Its module port derives from Nxer and contributors'
Twist Space Technology, revision f59f874694906144e205a3451dfd665c701eca97:
https://github.com/Nxer/Twist-Space-Technology-Mod
The corresponding adapted sources and twelve copied texture/animation files are
GPL-3.0-only. The complete license is provided in GPL-3.0.txt.

Parallel, speed and power-consumption parameter hatches (T1-T8), including
static/dynamic parallel and speed variants, retain their 40 assembler recipes.
Three overclock controllers and their recipes are also ported. Per-overclock
duration/EU ratios match GTNG: low-speed 4/4, perfect 8/4, singularity 8/2.
At the user's request, the singularity recipe researches and consumes one
GT-Never-Reset Perfect Overclock Controller instead of GTNG's Perfect Execution
Core, in addition to its original 2048 Perfect Overclock Controllers. The
remaining ingredients, 192-hour research, 30 EU/t scanning power, MAX recipe
voltage and 64-day recipe duration are unchanged.
Upstream controller types, execution-core hatches, wireless execution and
machine structures are not included. GT-Never-Reset's
controller uses numbered recipe slots with a live, subclass-defined thread cap.

Automatic tooltips are adapted from LineBroke and contributors' GT-Not-Leisure
PR #654 via GT-Not-Good's detached-instance adaptation, originally pinned to
1cd98b4f61e4c77f65263171d3f03dda9c813382:
https://github.com/ABKQPO/GT-Not-Leisure/pull/654
The merged PR head a6ee80aea6eaf98e2478e847a96dfdc3a620847b was inspected on
2026-10-27. Description styling follows
8f3aaa38713b8b085338cb676b66cbd01c58875d: Markdown controls its own italics.
The tooltip sources retain LGPL-3.0-only notices. LGPL-3.0.txt and GPL-3.0.txt
provide the complete license texts. No GTNL artwork is copied.

The adaptation reads normal performance, Markdown description variables,
fixed and tiered structure pieces, dimensions, hollow space, controller position,
material tiers, hatch requirements and structure channels. Independent shapes
have their own geometry and requirements. Cumulative shapes list added blocks
and their full structure's requirements. Repeating shapes list a base and the
materials added per layer, with height, length or width ranges. Pure count hooks
also record per-layer hatches and shared casing totals.
Structure support follows the merged PR head
a6ee80aea6eaf98e2478e847a96dfdc3a620847b. Projector sizes 1 through 64 are
sampled on detached controllers, without invoking checkMachine. Repeated shapes
reuse a canonical structure definition; material sub-channel probes preserve
the other channels, and custom channel objects retain their descriptions.
GTNL-specific gameplay changes, machine migrations, audit/export commands and
steam/wireless special cases are excluded.
Voltage/heat probing is not ported; GT-Never-Reset uses explicit performance
stats. New machines can follow scripts/qa/StructureTooltip-NOTES.md for the
structure and pure count interfaces. QA fixtures are not part of the mod JAR.

These third-party portions are outside the project's general MIT grant.
SOURCE_MANIFEST.json records source and destination hashes and modifications.
