Prototype generator; balance values and structure are temporary. Controller available in creative mode.
Consumes 1 L each of deuterium and tritium per second at full power; produces 1 L of helium.
Rated net generation: {var:power} EU/t. External ignition energy: {var:ignition} EU.
Input IC2 coolant; output IC2 hot coolant. Maximum coolant flow: {var:coolant} L/s.
Hot coolant output blockage prevents cooling. Helium output blockage pauses fuel loading.
Warning at {var:warning} K limits power to 25%; emergency shutdown at {var:scram} K.
Reaching {var:explosion} K causes an explosion. Shutdown does not remove residual heat!
Restart after emergency shutdown requires cooling below the warning temperature and pressing Ignite.
Generated EU is sent directly to the placing player's wireless energy network; no dynamo hatch is used.
