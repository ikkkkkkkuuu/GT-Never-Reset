Every process finds its place within chaos.

Credentials select routes; structure modules provide processing capabilities. Supports {var:groups} groups, {var:modules} modules and {var:steps} steps per chain.

Use presets or AE processing patterns, ordered left to right and top to bottom. Patterns are retained; stack size sets the step repetition count.

Groups retain intermediate materials independently. All remaining materials are exported at the end. The two positions below each credential hatch are dedicated inputs; main inputs are shared.

Power loss, shutdown and incomplete structures preserve work. Export waits for the current step to finish, then stops the group and exports its cache; paused steps must resume to finish. Credentials and installed modules cannot be removed while work remains; missing modules can still be added.

Structure modules currently package ordinary GT single-block machines in supported recipe maps without special processing conditions. AE chains reject special-condition recipes, random outputs and ambiguous matches.
