Runs bricked blast furnace recipes and consumes the same fuel. Requires no power or maintenance.
The main kiln runs on its own. One auxiliary furnace increases processing speed to {var:one_auxiliary_speed}x, and two increase it to {var:two_auxiliaries_speed}x. Speed is checked when each recipe starts.
Use a stack of 1, 2, or 3 controllers to preview the main kiln, one auxiliary furnace, or both auxiliary furnaces.
