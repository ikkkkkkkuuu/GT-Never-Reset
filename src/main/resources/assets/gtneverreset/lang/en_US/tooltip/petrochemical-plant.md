Supply crude oil or natural gas with hydrogen and steam. The plant automatically distills, desulfurizes, cracks, and redistills its feedstocks.
Naphtha uses light steam cracking and refinery gas uses severe steam cracking. Intermediate fluids accumulate inside the plant until there is enough for the next batch.
Distillation and cracking use standard overclocks. Chemical reactions use perfect overclocks. The cupronickel coils reduce cracking EU/t by {var:cracking_discount}%.
Distillation products leave through the shared outputs. Filter them by fluid or use an ME Output Hatch. Solid byproducts need an Output Bus.
