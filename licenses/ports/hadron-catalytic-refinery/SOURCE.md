# Hadron Catalytic Refinery

The structure is adapted from [GTL-Enhancedcore](https://github.com/hongshaotimo/GTL-Enhancedcore/tree/41f3a122bf01fb405fd4f33e161642a95503a7e4) under the GNU LGPL v3. The license text is included in the sibling directory `universal-joint-factory-port/LICENSE.txt`.

The source asset is `src/main/resources/data/gtl_enhancedcore/structures/hollow/hadron_catalytic_refinery.pattern.gz`, SHA-256 `d91aacf9a536c56260bafc1654b7847feeb00b84a494ccfa79c371c495eab073`. The MBS1 conversion reverses the UP and FRONT axes, preserves spaces and replaces the controller C with ~ at (8, 22, 0). Dimensions are 17 × 25 × 17.

GTNH uses assembler casings, chemical-grade glass, iron blocks, stable titanium casings, robust tungstensteel casings and nichrome coils. The front Q/X positions accept hatches. Recipe types follow the upstream processing registration. GTNH splits extraction into item and fluid maps, and the non-cell chemical dehydrator map supplies the evaporation function. The nine controller ingredients, solder volume, IV assembler voltage and processing time follow the upstream equipment recipe.
