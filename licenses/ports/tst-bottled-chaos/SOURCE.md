# Twist Space Technology: Bottled Chaos port

Source: https://github.com/Nxer/Twist-Space-Technology-Mod

Revision: a4b6e17bb02802edfd5c449248e20881faee25b8

The Bottled Chaos processing, preset chains, structure packaging, GUI, related utility code, structure data and textures are adapted from this revision under GPL-3.0. Copyright remains with the original authors and contributors. The accompanying GPL-3.0.txt is included in distributions.

GT-Never-Reset adaptations: separate packages and registration, independent components, credential-free route selection, native GTNR modules and AE integration.

The `assets/gtneverreset/multiblock/chaos_sources/` MBS1 resources contain 46 converted structure arrays from the 29 source machines referenced by Bottled Chaos. `BottledChaosNativeProfiles` adapts their structure element mappings and source parameters to native GTNH controllers and locally registered components. Manufacturing recipe adaptations replace TST-only infrastructure as documented in `docs/bottle-in-chaos.md`.
