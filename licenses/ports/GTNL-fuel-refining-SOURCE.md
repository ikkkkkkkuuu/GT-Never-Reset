The Bottle in Chaos fuel presets adapt the rocket fuel and naquadah fuel recipes from GT-Not-Leisure's `FuelRefiningComplexRecipes.java` at revision `6d650c6bde0a82b15ed5d0e91ff95cbe4b745519`.

GTNR maps rocket fuel processing to its packaged vacuum furnace source and naquadah fuel processing to its packaged naquadah refinery source. The upstream license is included in [GTNL-LICENSE.txt](GTNL-LICENSE.txt).
