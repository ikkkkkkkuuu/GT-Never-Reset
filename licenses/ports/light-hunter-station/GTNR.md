# GTNR Light Hunter station

The station and satellite structures, controller faces, recipes, and star renderer were adapted from GT-Not-Good. Its source and asset records identify the pinned GTLAdditions, GTLCore, GregTech Modern and GTL pack versions. The two geometries are stored in MBS1 after applying GT-Not-Good's row and aisle reversal. The GTNR namespace contains the regular star model, thirteen planet skins, beam shaders and selected structure-casing artwork. The former full-array model and thread modifier are omitted.

The original station and satellite controller sources and Light Hunter assembly-line recipes carry GPL-3.0-only. The renderer also incorporates the camera offset calculation from the GT-Not-Good HeartBlackHoleMath class. Additional casing artwork and recipes retain the terms in the corresponding source notices under sources/. The original manifests document the historical source paths. The rights to these materials remain with their respective authors.

GTNR changes include owner-grid upkeep and satellite supply, independent satellite grades, per-parallel voltage configuration, connected casing integration, resource paths and the GTNR assembly-line upgrade recipes.
