FusionPowerPlantGui adapts the compact controller layout, palette, badge/bar/gauge
painting and stepper approach from:
- src/main/java/com/nateyburr/beyondthestars/machines/gui/BTSControllerGui.java
- src/main/java/com/nateyburr/beyondthestars/machines/gui/BTSGuiKit.java
- src/main/java/com/nateyburr/beyondthestars/machines/gui/KitDraw.java
- src/main/java/com/nateyburr/beyondthestars/machines/quasar/MTEQuasarEngineGui.java

The adaptation keeps only local GUI drawing helpers and uses GTNR's own fusion
state, localization, synchronization, controls and existing GregTech textures.
The reference's simulation, row serialization, recipes and assets are not ported.
