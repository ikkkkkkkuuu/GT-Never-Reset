# GregTech Lite Catalytic Reformer

The multiblock geometry, controller crafting recipe, reforming recipe data, and ZSM-5 synthesis are adapted from GregTech Lite Core under the Apache License 2.0. [Upstream source](https://github.com/GregTechLite/GregTech-Lite-Core/tree/3a8390739a5127059b19ebdf6123cad432f5d254)

- Structure and behavior in `MultiblockCatalyticReformer.kt`
- Controller crafting in `MachineRecipeLoader.kt`
- Reforming recipes and catalyst synthesis in `OilsChain.kt`

The Java port uses GTNH's StructureLib and GregTech APIs, the Large Chemical Reactor for catalyst synthesis, and GTNR's voltage-scaled recipe threads. The license and attribution are in `GregTech-Lite-Core-LICENSE.txt` and `GregTech-Lite-Core-NOTICE.txt`.
