All files except inset.png, list_row.png and progress.png are copied unchanged;
only the resource paths and filenames are adapted for Minecraft 1.7.10
(button_bar.png is vertical_buttons_bg.png). inset.png is an 8x9 crop of the
list well in networkstatus.png (columns 8-11 and 166-169, rows 38-42 and
114-117) so it can be drawn as a nine-slice. list_row.png is the 67x44 CPU row
sprite pair in cpu_selector.png (x 100-166, y 0-43). progress.png places the
empty progress well of inscriber.png (x 135-140, y 39-56) next to its full bar
(x 176-181, y 0-17).
The files in skin/ae2_dark are adaptations using the light-to-dark AE2 palette
of Modernity-GTNH-Dark-UI. The shared RGB mappings for nontransparent pixels are:
  #413F54 -> #0A090E
  #696D88 -> #14151D
  #878FA5 -> #1D2029
  #9A9FB4 -> #242631
  #ADB0C4 -> #2D2F3C
  #CBCCD4 -> #42434B
  #F2F2F2 -> #8E8E8E
The additional colour #4D4D67 becomes #0D0D13 in text_field.png and states.png,
and #0D0D12 in list_row.png and progress.png. Other colours, including the
selection blues and purple progress bar, and all alpha values are preserved.
The files in skin/ae2_dark_purple are copies of skin/ae2_dark with the selection
blues mapped to fluix purple:
  #9CD3FF -> #644595
  #DAFFFF -> #E2A3E3
  #708CBA -> #212040
  #517497 -> #212040 (checkbox.png only)
The mapping covers button_highlighted.png, checkbox.png, list_row.png,
slider_fill.png and slider_tag.png, the hovered button and tab frames of
states.png (x 192-231, y 128-148 and x 160-182, y 224-246) and two selected
cells of legacy_states.png (x 112-127, y 208-223 and x 208-223, y 240-255).
Icon pixels in the same blues are preserved.
slider_track.png, slider_fill.png, slider_tag.png and divider.png in all skin
folders are not AE2 files; they are small sprites drawn for GTNLLib in the AE2
palette.
